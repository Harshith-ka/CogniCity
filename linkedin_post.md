🏙️ I built a city that thinks.

Not a game. Not a dashboard with charts pretending to be "smart." An actual living, breathing digital twin — thousands of autonomous AI citizens with memory, personality, relationships, jobs, and free will, all running inside one simulation.

Here's what's actually happening under the hood:

🧠 Every citizen is a LangGraph agent — Perceive → Decide → Act → Reflect — with LLM-powered decisions (OpenAI/Anthropic/Ollama), episodic + semantic memory, and Big-5 personality traits driving how they behave.

🌆 A full economy, government, and social fabric — 6 autonomous government departments make real policy decisions, citizens gossip and post on social media, elections run with personality-driven voter modeling, and a real economy tracks jobs, salaries, and businesses.

🌋 Disasters, pandemics, weather — 7 disaster types with physics-inspired damage curves, a full SEIR pandemic model with lockdowns and vaccination, dynamic weather that actually affects citizen health and traffic.

🏗️ A real 3D city, not a map — built in Three.js, PBR-rendered, where I can walk the streets, drop a new hospital or bridge with a live AI-scored impact analysis before committing, and watch it get built over simulation ticks.

💼 And then I turned it into an actual SaaS platform — multi-tenant auth, role-based access, a 5-tier plan catalog with real entitlement enforcement, a credits ledger, self-service signup, an admin portal, and a React Native mobile app so you can run all of this from your phone.

🧪 The part I'm most proud of: an Agent Evaluation Sandbox where you can upload your own ONNX model and stress-test it against correlated synthetic populations — robustness, fairness, adversarial failure rate, all scored automatically.

Stack: FastAPI, PostgreSQL, Redis, Neo4j, Qdrant, LangGraph, Streamlit, Three.js, React Native/Expo, Docker.

This started as "let me simulate a few hundred citizens" and turned into a full platform. Still building.
